## 🤖 Shanti Science, ICT & Robotics Club (SSIRC)

As Marketing Head, I help tell the story of SSIRC: **Innovate. Create. Inspire.**

SSIRC is a student club at Shree Shanti Namuna Secondary School in Manigram, Rupandehi, Nepal, where students learn programming, electronics and robotics by building real things through workshops, competitions and team projects.

🔗 [Visit the website](https://shantisirc.com) · [Events](https://shantisirc.com/events.html) · [Meet the team](https://shantisirc.com/team.html)
